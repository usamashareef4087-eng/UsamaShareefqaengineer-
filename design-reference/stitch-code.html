Software issues shouldn't become business problems.

When testing is rushed or unstructured, critical defects bypass internal review and degrade customer trust in production. Systematic QA solves root causes before rollout.

01

Bugs Discovered Too Late

Issues caught after deployment cost 10x more to remediate, frustrate paying customers, and pull core engineers away from roadmap deliverables.

Structured cross-platform matrices

Clear quality gates & executive sign-offs
